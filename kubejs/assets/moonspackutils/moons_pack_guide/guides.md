# Guides

This modpack contains a lot of mods! It can be very bewildering when you're just getting started.
This page will give you some advice about where to start as well as listing all the guide-books for the different mods
and how to get them.

---

## Where to Start

The core mod around which most of this modpack is built is [Modern Industrialization](https://modrinth.com/mod/modern-industrialization).
It is recommended to start out with this mod until you have set up <ItemLink id="modern_industrialization:analog_circuit"/> production.

It is also highly recommended to look into [Applied Energistics 2](https://modrinth.com/mod/ae2), which will make 
managing your storage and item transport much less of a headache. A couple of tech mods add their own digital storage,
but they may be more expensive and less compatible.

From here you can branch into:
- [RailCraft](https://modrinth.com/mod/railcraft-reborn); low-cost, high speed, electrified railways for long distance transport.
- [Mekanism](https://modrinth.com/mod/mekanism); better ore processing, little robot buddies, big nuclear reactors!
- [Oritech](https://modrinth.com/mod/oritech); cool animated machines, automated enchanting, particle accelerators!
- [Psi](https://modrinth.com/mod/psi); use technology to become a wizard!
- [Computercraft](https://modrinth.com/mod/cc-tweaked); for the real nerds, automate your production lines with LUA code! (turtles are disabled sorry)
- [Hostile Neural Networks](https://modrinth.com/mod/hostile-neural-networks); automated and lag-less mod farming!
- [Sync](https://modrinth.com/mod/neosync); clone yourself!

---

## Helpful Ideas

For building, look at [Only Hammers](https://modrinth.com/mod/only-hammers) and [Portable Blueprints](https://modrinth.com/mod/portable-blueprints).

For getting around, look at Railcraft and [Immersive Aircraft](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersive-aircraft).

For much cheaper item and fluid pipes, check out [Simple Belts](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-conveyor-belts) or
[Modern Dynamics](https://modrinth.com/mod/modern-dynamics).

For early game teleporting check out [Dimensional Doors](https://modrinth.com/mod/dimensionaldoors).

For early game storage check out [Storage Drawers](https://modrinth.com/mod/storage-drawers).

---

## Late Game

This modpack really tries to build up to a satisfying late game, so you have something cool to do with your giant fusion reactor.
If you want to have a peek at what there is to look forward to check out:
- [Replication](https://modrinth.com/mod/replication); Replicating items and resources infinitely!
- [Tempad](https://modrinth.com/mod/tempad); Highly advanced teleportation!
- [Draconic Evolution](https://modrinth.com/mod/draconic-evolution); extremely powerful gear, massive energy storage, time and weather control.
- [Avaritia](https://modrinth.com/mod/avaritianeo); god-like armour and tools.
- And some trinkets like the [PortalGun](https://modrinth.com/mod/portal-gun), [Orbital Railgun](https://modrinth.com/mod/orbital-railgun)

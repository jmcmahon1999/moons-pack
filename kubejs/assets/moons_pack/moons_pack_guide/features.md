# Modpack Features

The modpack also has a few custom features which won't be in any of the mods' documentation.

## Freezing

If you find yourself in cold regions, without warm enough clothing, you will start to freeze.
You can stop this by finding shelter and warmth (torchlight, fire etc.). 

The coldness of an area is derived from its biome and its altitude.

Leather armour, as well as more technologically advanced armours, will enable 
you to climb to higher altitudes and survive colder biomes without freezing.

## PortalTech

PortalTech is an original mod developed for this modpack. It disallows the usual methods
of opening portals and replaces them with multiblock machines.

## Ore Generation

Certain modded ores have been biome restricted. 

- Quartz Ore will now spawn throughout the overworld.
- Antimony Ore will spawn in mountainous or jungle biomes.
- Bauxite Ore will spawn in wet or underwater biomes as well as Mediterranean biomes.
- Monazite Ore will spawn in arctic or arid-desert biomes.
- Tungsten Ore will spawn in hilly or mountainous biomes.
- Uranium Ore will spawn in dry biomes.

Vanilla ore generation is unchanged.

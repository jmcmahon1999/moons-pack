# Modpack Planning

Planning and ideas for the Moons-Pack modpack.

## Core Concepts

### Research Mechanic

Villager-like NPCs the player employs to conduct "research". The research takes time, and the researchers require some upkeep.
Research outputs unique uncraftable items, which are used but not consumed in machines or crafting. 
Research can also output "documentation", written pages describing the shape of multiblock machines, hidden crafting recipes or the source of rare items.
Research time could scale with the level of the technology and require the researchers to be located in specific dimensions or biomes.
Upkeep can be in the form of energy to computers or machines the researchers are "using" as well as food or goods which the researchers consume from an inventory.

Implementation Ideas:
- Researcher Mob
    - Could be villager based; simple, gives obvious route to acquiring researcher; default villager behaviour/job system might not be workable.
    - Could be custom NPC; complex, where does the player get these NPCs; custom behaviour e.g. multiple researchers using the same "receptacle".
    - Picks up item to initiate a "research task" phase. This phase takes a fixed duration.
- Datapad Item
    - Craftable Item requiring multiple crafted components. (encourages auto crafting)
    - Could be tiered, providing simple gating for more complex "research".
    - Alternatively tiering could improve duration of Researcher task.
    - Used by Researchers in research phase.
    - Deposited into "receptacle" and consumed, adding progress to current research.


### Portal Opening

Portals for dimensions such as the Nether, End and Aether should be gated behind tech/research.
Opening the portals should require multiblock machines which need to be powered and supplied with necessary materials.
As much as requiring upkeep for the portal to remain open seems interesting, Minecraft portals are buggy and laggy, so keeping them open permanently might be better.
The cost to open the portal is probably best as a large one-time energy drain or expensive resources.
